How to use Evenly

Evenly is a simple way to split payments within groups. Here’s how you use it:

1. One user creates a group, and the rest join using the group code visible on the group page. The creator of the group is automatically a member.

2. During the course of the group’s adventure, everyone adds what they’ve paid and specifies who they split it with.

3. Once everyone has paid, the debt will be evenly split. When all debts are settled, the group is deactivated.

4. You can view the group's history from your profile page.
