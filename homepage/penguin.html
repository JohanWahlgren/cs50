Penguin

Penguins are flightless birds primarily found in the Southern Hemisphere, particularly in Antarctica. They are highly adapted for life in the water, with streamlined bodies and flipper-like wings that make them excellent swimmers. Most penguin species are known for their distinctive black and white coloration, often described as tuxedo-like. Penguins are social animals and live in large colonies, where they engage in complex social behaviors and vocalizations. They primarily feed on fish, squid, and krill, using their excellent diving abilities to catch prey underwater..

Did you know?

Penguins are incredible swimmers, capable of reaching speeds of up to 15 miles per hour (24 km/h) in the water!
