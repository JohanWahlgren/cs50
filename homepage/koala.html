Koala

Koalas are marsupials native to Australia, known for their tree-dwelling lifestyle and adorable appearance. They primarily inhabit eucalyptus forests and feed almost exclusively on eucalyptus leaves, which are toxic to most other animals but provide the koalas with necessary nutrients. Koalas are nocturnal, spending most of their day sleeping, typically for up to 18 hours, to conserve energy. They have a unique vocalization system, with males producing deep, bellowing calls to attract females during mating season. Due to habitat loss and disease, koalas are classified as vulnerable, and conservation efforts are ongoing to protect their natural habitats and ensure their survival.

Did you know?

Koalas have fingerprints that are remarkably similar to human fingerprints. In fact, they are so close that they can confuse crime scene investigators.
